Earthwork factors

Soil presets fill in shrink and swell from FHWA Project Development & Design Manual, Exhibit 5.1 A. FHWA notes these are averages and can vary ±33%. Use your geotech or compaction report when you have one. Typing your own numbers switches the soil type to Custom. A negative shrink means the material bulks up even after compaction (rocky material).

How the numbers are figured
  • Cut/fill at a shot. Cut is positive, fill is negative.
    Laser: cut/fill = target rod − rod reading (rod reads shorter than target = high ground = cut).
    Elevations: cut/fill = existing elev − finish grade elev.
  • Surface. Shots are joined into non-overlapping triangles (Delaunay triangulation, the same TIN surface method used in civil design software). Cut/fill is treated as varying in a straight line across each triangle.
  • Volume. Each triangle: area × average of its 3 corner depths. A triangle that crosses grade is split on the 0.00 line so cut and fill are counted separately. CY = cu ft ÷ 27.
  • Average depths are area-weighted: avg cut = cut volume ÷ cut area. Bunched-up shots don't skew it the way a plain average would.
  • Shrink/swell. bank CY needed for fill = compacted fill CY ÷ (1 − shrink), loose CY = bank CY × (1 + swell), loads = loose CY ÷ truck load, rounded up.
  • Balance grade. The page moves the whole finish grade up or down until cut equals shrink-adjusted fill and reports the change.
  • Coverage. Volumes only count the area inside your outermost shots. Shoot the pad corners and edges to cover the whole pad.
