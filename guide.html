Pad Cut/Fill Mapper User guide

Shoot a pad with a laser and rod (or off stakes or elevations), tap each shot onto the plan, and get cut/fill at every shot, average depths, volumes in cubic yards, and truckloads.

Quick start: no benchmark, laser and rod

  1. Hit New pad. Enter the pad width and length in feet.
  2. Under How are you shooting it? pick Laser + rod, no benchmark. Leave Grade rod from on Average of shots.
  3. Set the laser up anywhere it can see the whole pad. No benchmark needed.
  4. Walk the pad in a rough grid. At each spot, tap that spot on the plan and type the rod reading. Press Enter (or Done) and tap the next spot.
  5. Shoot the corners and edges too. Volumes only count the area inside your outer shots.
  6. When the whole pad is shot, hit Done adding. The labels flip from rod readings to cut/fill.
  7. Read the Grade rod in Pad setup. Set your grade rod to that number and cut to it.

Grade rod = all readings added up ÷ number of shots. Cut/fill at each shot = grade rod − that shot's reading. Don't trust the cut/fills until every shot is in; the average changes with each one.

Buttons above the plan

Cut/Fill · Shot readings
What the dots are labeled with: cut/fill (C 0.45' / F 0.30') or the raw reading you entered. Dot color is always red = cut, blue = fill.
Ft & tenths · Ft-in
Decimal feet (5.50', engineer rod) or feet-inches to the nearest 1/8" (5' 6", inch rod). Changes every number on the page. In Ft-in, the rod reading boxes become ft / in / eighths. Elevations always stay in decimal feet.
Done adding · + Add shots
Done adding locks the plan: tapping a shot shows its info, and nothing gets added or moved by accident. + Add shots turns adding back on.
Snap to 1 ft
Rounds shot positions to the nearest foot.
Undo
Takes back the last add, move, delete or clear.
Clear shots
Deletes every shot on this pad. Tap twice to confirm.

Shooting modes

Laser + rod, no benchmark
Enter rod readings only. The page sets the grade rod for you. Average of shots = sum ÷ count, the field method. Area-weighted balance corrects for uneven shot spacing and for shrink, so cut exactly makes the fill. Raise / lower pad moves the grade up (+) or down (−) from there.
Laser + rod readings
You already know the target rod reading at finish grade. Enter it once, then a reading at each shot. Cut/fill = target − reading.
Cut/fill numbers
Enter cut or fill straight off grade stakes or a grade checker.
Ground elevations
Enter finish grade elevation, then existing ground elevation at each shot. Cut/fill = existing − finish. With no benchmark, call a solid object on site 100.00 and shoot everything off it.

Entering and fixing shots

  • Add: tap empty space on the plan (adding must be on). The shot box opens; type the reading.
  • Open a shot: tap its dot, or tap its row in the Shot log.
  • Close without adding: with a shot open, tapping empty space just closes it.
  • Move: drag the dot (adding on), or type exact X / Y feet. X is from the left edge, Y from the top edge.
  • Note: label a shot, e.g. "NE corner" or "stake 4".
  • Delete shot: in the shot box.
  • A dot with "?" has no reading yet and is left out of the math.

Reading the results

Cut / Fill (CY)
In-place cut yards and compacted fill yards, with the square footage each covers.
Avg cut depth / Avg fill depth
Area-weighted: cut volume ÷ cut area. Bunched-up shots don't skew it.
Average over pad
Net depth across the whole covered area. C means the pad is high overall, F means low.
Plain average of shots
Straight average of all cut/fills. Close to zero in average-of-shots mode.
Area covered by shots
Percent of the pad inside your outer shots. Under 100% means some volume isn't counted. Shoot the corners.
To balance
How far to raise or lower finish grade so cut makes the fill after shrink.

Earthwork factors

  • Soil type fills in shrink and swell from FHWA PDDM Exhibit 5.1 A. These are averages; FHWA notes ±33% variation. Use your geotech or compaction numbers when you have them. Typing your own numbers switches the soil type to Custom.
  • Shrink %: bank (in place) to compacted. Negative means the material bulks up even when compacted (rocky material).
  • Swell %: bank to loose in the truck.
  • Truck load (loose CY): what one truck hauls. Used for load counts.
  • Strip depth (in): topsoil stripped over the whole pad, in bank CY and loads.
  • The panel shows bank CY needed to make the fill, import or export in bank and loose CY, and truckloads.

Saving jobs

  • Type a Job name and hit Save job. Reopen it from Saved jobs.
  • Jobs save in the browser on this device only. A different phone, browser, or link (Claude link vs GitHub link) has its own separate list.
  • The pad you're working on is kept automatically if you close the page.
  • Copy summary copies the results and shot list as CSV. Paste into Excel or a text.

Field tips

  • Shoot a rough grid (every 20–25 ft) plus every corner and edge midpoint.
  • Add extra shots at humps, holes and grade breaks. Uneven spacing is fine with Area-weighted balance.
  • Keep the laser in one setup for the whole pad. If you move it, every reading after the move is on a different height and the average is off.
  • Check your rod is plumb and the receiver is locked before each shot.
